Education

Our policy for Education

Making Education Accessible for Everyone.

At the Unity Party, we believe education is a human right and should be accessible to everyone.

Recent census data revealed that 34,700 children missed out on an education. We want to tackle the root causes, starting in the home.

In 2024, 31% of children in the UK were living in poverty. Poverty can also affect a child's access to education.

  • Schools receive £2.61 per child for free school meals, while meals cost an average of £2.91. We want to increase funding for school meals and all school departments.
  • We want children to have access to free and nutritional meals in school if they are unable to be provided by parents or carers.
  • We will also provide support for carers of children to buy the correct uniform or equipment to ensure every child has the essential equipment for school.
  • We will fund and improve schools that specialise in SEN to ensure if a child has difficulties or differences, they are still able to access the rightful education.

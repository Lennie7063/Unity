Our policy for Culture

Celebrating Our Culture and Diversity

Culture is very important to us at the Unity Party as it shapes the way people live their day-to-day lives. We believe people should not face discrimination because of their religion, race, gender or sexuality.

We will fight to continue using the European Convention on Human Rights, including through Article 14 (the non-discrimination clause) and The Human Rights Act.

  • Continue education about different cultures to tackle prejudice and discrimination.
  • Protect people from discrimination based on their religion, race, gender or sexuality.
  • Fight and Campaign for equal rights for trans, non-binary and any other gender identity.
  • Preserve British history and heritage by supporting heritage sites and museums.
  • Completely close the pay gap between men and women.

Food/Farming

Our policy for Food/Farming

Protecting our Farms

Farming is estimated to bring about £15B to the UK annually contributing massively to our economy. It is also a way of life, work and sustenance for many people around the country.

We want to protect farmers and farming while also making sure our animals live happy and healthy lives.

We will work with farmers to create a more humane, productive and greener way of farming:

  • Provide farmers with equipment or financial aid to switch to greener and more humane farming methods.
  • Work to ban harmful chemicals that may harm wildlife or leak into rivers.
  • Ensure surplus food can be distributed to people who need it, rather than going to waste.
  • Creating more parks, playgrounds, skate parks and recreational spaces to encourage young people to enjoy the outdoors and spend time with friends and family.

Animals Wellbeing

  • Improve living conditions so animals can live fulfilled lives rather than being kept in cramped conditions.
  • Increase farming spending by 49%, from £2.4 billion to £3.5 billion, to support better animal welfare and farming conditions.

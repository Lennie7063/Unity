Our policy for Community

Building Stronger Communities

Community plays a big part in our party. Your voice is what matters most to us. We want to ensure everyone, no matter where they come from, feels welcome, accepted, and free to be themselves.

To achieve this, we will invest in stronger communities by:

  • Increasing investment in community sporting events, including marathons and other local activities.
  • Supporting more parades and celebrations, including Pride events in major cities across the UK.
  • Ensuring everyone's voice is heard by funding more open debates and public discussions.
  • Creating more parks, playgrounds, skate parks and recreational spaces to encourage young people to enjoy the outdoors and spend time with friends and family.

These improvements will help reduce anti-social behaviour, create safer neighbourhoods, and bring people together.

One united community is at the heart of the Unity Party, and together we can build a stronger, fairer future for everyone.

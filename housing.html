Housing

Our policy for Housing

Making sure everyone has a home

At the unity party we believe that housing, and a place to live that can provide you with what you need is a basic human right. We are strong believers that no one should have to worry about inadequate housing or shelter.

In 2025, it is estimated that 4,800 people in England were sleeping rough on any given night, and in 2023, 299,000 households qualified for help due to the risk of homelessness. This does not include the households that did not ask for help from their local government. This means that, for every 1,000 households, 28 qualified for help due to the risk of homelessness.

  • A £5 billion investment will ensure that homes are well insulated for colder winters while also improving ventilation and making air conditioning units more affordable for households, helping people adapt to increasingly frequent hotter summers.
  • We will use local government initiatives to make support more accessible to those who are struggling to find or maintain housing. This may be due to financial difficulties, mental health issues, or overcrowding.
  • A £15 billion investment will fund the construction of new, affordable homes across the UK, helping more people access safe, secure, and affordable housing.

Everyone deserves the security of knowing they have a place to call home.

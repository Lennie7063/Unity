Transport

Our policy for Transport

Making Transport More Affordable and Sustainable

Public transport is the only way for many people to travel, with many still struggling to afford it. However, we also know it is the UK's largest producer of greenhouse gases, at an average of 30% of all gas emissions.

We want to make public transport cheaper and more accessible while also working towards a greener transport system.

We will work to make public transport more affordable, greener and accessible:

  • Make tickets cheaper for everyone.
  • Focus on free bus fares for under 18s and school children.
  • Strive for the electrification of public transport, reducing gas emissions and travelling costs.
  • Nationalise all major forms of public transport, this would take them aways from big corporations which normally only care about stakeholders and money.

Cycling and Walking

We will also strive to make our streets a safer place for those who may use cycling or walking as forms of transport.

  • Create more accessible footpaths and cycle paths.
  • Improve rural areas, giving them better access to towns or places they may need.

Aviation

As a form of transportation outside or into the country, many will choose the route of aviation. This brings in a lot of money to our economy, making tourism more accessible.

We want to support aviation while also working to make airports greener and more sustainable:

  • Strive to expand our airports, creating more access for tourism.
  • Adapt our airports to create a greener means of flying.
  • Switch to greener ways of travel, such as electric buses, to reduce gas emissions when travelling around the airports.
  • Introduce solar panels around or on airports to generate solar power as a greener energy source.

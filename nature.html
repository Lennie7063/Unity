Our policy for Nature

Protecting Britain's Wildlife

At the Unity Party, we view nature and wildlife as something vulnerable that must be protected, and we strive to do exactly that.

Nature is the foundation of any environment. The UK has beautiful and diverse ecosystems, habitats, and wildlife that we believe should be protected.

We will work hard to protect the current laws already in place that safeguard wildlife and habitats, including the Conservation of Habitats and Species Regulations 2010, which protect species and their habitats by making it an offence to destroy the breeding sites or homes of protected and endangered animals.

  • Introduce a total ban on animal hunting for sport.
  • Remove terms such as "game birds", which classify certain animals as acceptable to hunt for human sport.
  • Ban the hunting of animals purely for recreation, as we believe it is inhumane.
  • Introduce a total ban on horse racing as part of our commitment to animal welfare.

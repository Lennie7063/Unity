Healthcare

Our policy for Healthcare

Restoring the National Health Service (NHS)

In 1948 the NHS was founded. Within the NHS approximately 1 million people are treated every 36 hours.

The NHS was born out of an ideology that healthcare should be available to every who needs it with no discrimination.

However, The NHS is collapsing. Waiting list are shooting through the roof and is at risk of being privatised by the right parties and invested into by billionaires who only care about money not the care of patients.

We will:

  • Reduce NHS waiting lists by investing in staff and improving efficiency.
  • Improve access to GP appointments by recruiting more doctors and making sure you can see one ASAP or the same day if urgent.
  • Restore access to NHS dentists.
  • Support NHS staff with better pay, improved working conditions, and greater investment in training and wellbeing.
  • Protect the founding principle of free healthcare at the point of use.

Green Energy

Our policy for Green Energy

Phasing out fossil fuels

In the UK the current push to a cleaner, greener way of life and energy is not enough for the rapidly growing issue that is climate change.

Climate change is acting more aggressive than ever and we need to combat that to ensure a safe future for our future generations and the planet itself.

We want to push green energy sources which are clean and renewable. This is to overall achieve net 0 by 2030, The unity party will:

  • Rely heavily on wind energy, this includes onshore wind (producing 60W) and offshore wind (producing around 90W).
  • More solar farms producing major amounts of energy (around 150W).
  • We will phase out the use of fossil fuels by 2035 by increasing the methods of removing carbon dioxide from the atmosphere using things like carbon capture, ect.

Together we believe making Britain a greener country can have a worldwide, lasting impact on the health of our planet.
